DESKTOP APPLICATION2026

QQ GPT Auto Reply.

GPT-assisted replies, with room to step in.

A local Python desktop app that connects a QQ bot through Tencent’s official SDK and uses the Codex CLI to draft replies. It supports direct messages to the bot and group @mentions, waiting 60 seconds before reply generation. Human replies sent through its control window cancel pending automated replies.

Automatic replies are off by default. Live QQ delivery has not yet been verified.
